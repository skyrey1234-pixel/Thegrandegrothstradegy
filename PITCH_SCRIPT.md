# The Grande Pitch Script

**This is the exact script to follow when presenting to the owner(s). Memorize the key points. Be conversational, not robotic.**

---

## OPENING (2 minutes)

*You're sitting down with the owner(s). Be genuine and direct.*

---

**"Hey, thanks for taking the time. I'm going to be direct with you: I was at The Grande recently, and I saw something most people miss.**

**You've got a $300K+ venue in one of the best locations in Atlantic Beach. The product is solid—your concept clearly works. But you're sitting at 50–60% capacity most days, and right now, you're losing money on a daily basis.**

**I'm not here to tell you what you're doing wrong. I'm here to show you exactly what you're leaving on the table, and how to turn that into cash in the next 90 days.**

**Is that something you're interested in?**"

*Pause. Let them respond. If yes, continue. If no or hesitant, say: "I have 30 minutes. Let me just walk you through what I'm seeing. You might surprise yourself."*

---

## THE PROBLEM (3 minutes)

*Pull up the interactive pitch deck on your laptop. Start at Slide 2.*

---

**"Okay, so first: your website. I spent 10 minutes on it yesterday, and here's what I found.**

**No cocktail menu with pricing. No photos showing the vibe. No reviews widget. Social media not linked. Event times are vague. No way to reserve a table. No incentive to join your email list.**

**Standard hospitality benchmark: when a customer can't see your menu or photos online, you lose 25–30% of walk-in conversions. That means 15–18 customers a night are finding you and leaving.**

**At $15 per customer, that's $6,750–8,100 you're losing every month.**

*Pause. Let that sink in.*

**My first move: fix the website. Week 1. Menu, photos, reviews widget, email automation, event pages—everything. That alone generates +$4.5K in additional revenue per month.**

**We literally pay for my entire month fee in week 1 just from fixing your website.**

*Slide to Slide 3. Show before/after.*

**Here's what before and after looks like. Here's the revenue impact: +15–18 new customers a night from people who were already coming to find you.**

**Does that make sense?**"

*Wait for acknowledgment. If they push back, go to OBJECTION HANDLING section.*

---

## THE OPPORTUNITY (4 minutes)

*Slide to Slide 5. Show the four revenue streams.*

---

**"Okay, so that's the quick win. But here's where it gets interesting.**

**I identified four revenue streams that we can activate over the next 90 days. Combined, they add $30K to $50K in new monthly revenue.**

**Stream 1: The Wait-List Redirect Program.**

*[Point to Slide 5]*

**I noticed you have two people standing outside your door. Literally 50 feet away, at Coop 303 and Flying Iguana, there are hundreds of people waiting 45 to 90 minutes for a table.**

**These are exactly your ideal customers. They want a drink. They're already in the area. They're bored while waiting. But they don't know you exist.**

**Your two staff members should not be standing there passively. They should be walking over every 10 minutes with a business card that says: 'Hey, waiting for a table? We have craft cocktails right around the corner. Show this at The Grande and get 20% off your first round.'**

**Conservative estimate: 40 to 60 customers a night from this alone. At $18–20 average spend. Boom: +$12K to $18K per month.**

**This is not a marketing budget—this is literally just your existing staff doing something different.**

**Stream 2: Ghost Kitchen Food Partnership.**

**Right now, people come in, order 1–2 drinks, then they get hungry and leave. If you had appetizers and small plates, they stay 30% longer and spend 40% more.**

**We partner with a local ghost kitchen or catering vendor—no kitchen overhead on your end. We handle menu development, pricing, supply chain. You keep 60–70% margin.**

**30% of customers order food = 20 extra customers a night × $6 margin each = +$8K to $12K per month.**

**Stream 3: Digital Marketing.**

**Right now your Instagram has 500 followers and no consistent posting strategy. We fix that. 4–5 posts per week. Paid ads targeting 25–45 demographic in a 5-mile radius. $500–800 per week budget.**

**Goal by Month 3: 2,000 followers, consistent engagement, 25–35 new repeat customers per week.**

**Revenue impact: +$6K to $10K per month from new customers who wouldn't have found you otherwise.**

**Stream 4: Daytime Activations.**

**Your venue is empty from 12 PM to 5 PM on weekdays. That's 5 empty hours a day. We partner with local yoga studios, gaming groups, pop-up markets—they host events here, bring their audiences, those audiences become your customers.**

**2–3 daytime events per week = +$4K to $8K per month, plus it fills a time gap where you currently have zero revenue.**

*Pause. Let them digest.*

**So here's the math:**
- *Website: +$4.5K*
- *Wait-list: +$12K–18K*
- *Food: +$8K–12K*
- *Digital + events: +$10K–18K*

**Total: +$34.5K to $52.5K per month in new revenue by Month 3.**

**You're currently at roughly $45K per month. In 90 days, you could be at $80K per month.**

**I charge $2,000 per month. You make that back in the first week from the website fix alone. Everything else is profit.**

*Pause. Make eye contact.*

**Does that resonate?**"

---

*[Point to Phase 1]*

*Make this promise clear.*

## HANDLING COMMON RESPONSES

### **"That sounds great, but I need to think about it."**

**Response:**

"I totally understand. But here's what I'd suggest: let's just start with the website. Week 1, no long-term commitment. You see the results yourself. By the end of Week 1, you've got a new menu online, 20 professional photos, review widgets, and email automation live. You're already getting more walk-ins.**

**Then we can decide if you want to go all-in on the other strategies. Deal?"**

*(This removes the barrier to entry. Once they see Week 1 results, they'll want Phase 2.)*

---

### **"Can't my staff handle this?"**

**Response:**

"Your staff could post on Instagram, but they're running the bar and making drinks. This is a full-time job. Social media is only 20% of what I do. I'm also negotiating restaurant partnerships, coordinating events, managing your email marketing, tracking analytics, and reporting monthly.**

**Your bartenders aren't going to walk outside and hand cards to people waiting at Coop 303. That's what I'm here for.**

**Think of it this way: they make you money in the bar. I make you money outside the bar. Different roles."**

---

### **"We tried marketing before and it didn't work."**

**Response:**

"I hear you. But here's the difference: I'm not just running ads and hoping something sticks. I'm taking a four-stream approach.**

**Stream 1 is your own customers—people who already know about you but didn't walk in (website fix). Stream 2 is your literal neighbors (wait-list program). Stream 3 is customers who want to stay longer (food partnership). Stream 4 is new audiences via digital.**

**The last place you were probably weak was having all four working together. I'm bringing all four at once.**

**Plus, the wait-list redirect program doesn't cost anything except your staff's time. It's pure revenue. If nothing else works, you at least hit that."**

---

### **"$2,000 a month is expensive."**

**Response:**

"I get it. But let's do the math. You need to bring in just 9 extra customers per week at $40 average spend to cover my fee.**

**That's 1.3 customers per day. Less than two.**

**The website fix alone brings in 15–18 customers per night. So we're hitting your payback threshold in the first week.**

**Everything after that is profit for you.**

**Plus, you're not just paying for marketing. You're paying for strategy, execution, and accountability. I'm betting on myself—if Month 1 doesn't hit baseline metrics, Month 2 is free.**

**I wouldn't offer that if I didn't know this works."**

---

### **"What if it doesn't work out?"**

**Response:**

"Great question. Here's my guarantee: if we're not seeing measurable results by the end of Month 1—more website traffic, more email signups, more door-staff card redemptions, something quantifiable—you don't pay for Month 2.**

**I'm confident enough in this plan that I'll work for free until we hit baseline.**

**But I will tell you: I'm not promising you'll make an extra $50K in Month 1. I'm promising we'll execute a proven strategy and measure exactly what works. By Month 3, all four streams should be live and generating new revenue.**

**If by Month 3 you're not seeing at least +$15K to $20K in new monthly revenue, we can part ways.**

**But I think you'll be surprised how fast this moves."**

---

### **"I need to talk to my partners."**

**Response:**

"Perfect. When can we all get on a call together? I can present this to all of you at once. I'll bring the interactive pitch deck and we can walk through it together.**

**How about [offer 2–3 times next week]? I'm flexible."**

*(Always try to include all decision-makers. Consensus closes faster.)*

## FINAL CHECKLIST BEFORE THE PITCH

- [ ] You've reviewed the interactive pitch deck (all 14 slides)
- [ ] You know the four revenue streams cold
- [ ] You have the website audit findings memorized
- [ ] You've visited The Grande and can speak specifically about the location/competition
- [ ] You know the owner's name and any details about them
- [ ] You have your contact info written down to hand them
- [ ] You have hard copies of the one-pager or business card
- [ ] You're ready to send the pitch deck link immediately after the meeting
- [ ] You're prepared for the top 3 objections above

---

## TONE & DELIVERY

**DO:**
- Be conversational, not scripted
- Show genuine interest in their business
- Use data to back up claims
- Make eye contact
- Let them talk (answer questions before moving on)
- Be confident but not arrogant

**DON'T:**
- Rush through the strategy
- Use jargon they don't understand
- Over-promise results
- Get defensive if they push back
- Forget to ask for the sale at the end

---

## THE ASK (Last 30 seconds)

After you've walked them through everything, be direct:

**"So here's what I need: I need you to say 'yes' to me starting on your website this week. That's phase one. We launch the full program in week 3. Cool?"**

*Then be quiet. Let them answer. Don't fill the silence.*

If they say yes, immediately follow up with:
**"Great. I'll send you a one-page contract and we can kick off Monday. I'll be in touch today with next steps. Sound good?"**

If they say they need to think about it:
**"No problem. But let's not let this sit. How about we start with just the website? Week 1. You see results. Then we decide on phase 2. Fair?"**

---

**Good luck. Go close them.** 🍹
