# Partnership Agreement Template

**For use with:** Wait-list redirect partners, ghost kitchen vendors, or daytime event co-hosts  
**Note:** This is a simple letter-of-intent style template. For formal contracts, consult a local attorney.

---

## Simple Partnership Agreement — The Grande Atlantic Beach

**Date:** [DATE]  
**Between:**  
- **The Grande Atlantic Beach** ("The Venue"), located at [address]  
- **[Partner Name/Business]** ("The Partner"), located at [address]

---

### Purpose

This agreement outlines a mutually beneficial partnership between The Venue and The Partner to drive customer traffic and increase revenue for both parties.

---

### Partnership Type

☐ **Wait-List Redirect** — Partner refers waiting guests to The Venue  
☐ **Ghost Kitchen / Food Service** — Partner provides food items sold at The Venue  
☐ **Daytime Event Co-Host** — Both parties co-host events at The Venue  
☐ **Other:** _______________________________________________

---

### Terms

#### For Wait-List Redirect Partners

1. Partner agrees to inform waiting guests that The Grande is available nearby as an option.
2. The Venue agrees to honor a **20% discount on the first round** for guests presenting a referral card.
3. The Venue will supply referral cards (printed) to Partner at no cost.
4. This arrangement is informal and either party may discontinue with 7 days' notice.
5. No financial exchange between parties — this is a goodwill referral program.

#### For Ghost Kitchen / Food Partners

1. Partner agrees to provide [X] menu items per agreed schedule.
2. Items will be prepared off-site and delivered to The Venue by [time] on service nights.
3. Pricing: The Venue pays Partner $[cost per item] per unit delivered.
4. The Venue retains the right to set menu prices for guests.
5. Minimum order: [X] units per service night.
6. Partner invoices The Venue weekly; payment due within 7 days of invoice.
7. Either party may terminate with 14 days' written notice.

#### For Event Co-Hosts

1. Both parties agree to promote the event via their respective social media channels.
2. Event date(s): [DATE(S)]
3. The Venue provides: venue space, bar service, and staff.
4. The Partner provides: [attendees / equipment / promotion / other].
5. Revenue split (if applicable): [X]% to The Venue, [X]% to The Partner.
6. Either party may cancel with 14 days' notice.

---

### Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep the terms of this agreement confidential and not share with competitors or third parties without written consent.

---

### Limitation of Liability

Neither party is liable for consequential, indirect, or punitive damages arising from this partnership. Total liability is limited to the value of services rendered in the month of the dispute.
