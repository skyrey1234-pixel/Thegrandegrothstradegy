# The Grande – Month-by-Month Deliverables

**Growth Manager:** Skyrey Trinidad  
**Client:** The Grande Atlantic Beach

## Phase 1: Website & Digital Foundation (Weeks 1–2)

### Week 1

- [ ] Hire photographer — schedule shoot for interior, bar, ambiance (20+ photos)
- [ ] Collect full cocktail menu with descriptions and pricing from bar manager
- [ ] Gather existing Yelp and Google reviews; export screenshots
- [ ] Claim and optimize Yelp business page (hours, photos, event info)
- [ ] Verify/claim Google Business Profile
- [ ] Upload professional photos to website (organized by section)
- [ ] Upload full cocktail menu with descriptions and prices

### Week 2

- [ ] Integrate Google Maps with clickable location pin
- [ ] Add Google and Yelp review widgets to homepage
- [ ] Link Instagram and TikTok in website header and footer
- [ ] Create individual event pages (Vinyl Night, Jazz Night, DJ Sets) with dates/times
- [ ] Integrate Resy or OpenTable for online table reservations
- [ ] Set up email signup with birthday incentive: "Get 15% off your birthday cocktail"
- [ ] Build email automation: birthday email sent 1 week before birthday
- [ ] Audit and fix mobile responsiveness
- [ ] Add CTAs: "Reserve a Table," "View Menu," "Follow on Instagram"
- [ ] **Milestone:** Website live — report baseline metrics (traffic, signups, reservation requests)

---

## Phase 2: Partnerships & Activation (Weeks 3–4)

### Week 3

- [ ] Brief and train door staff as brand ambassadors (script + discount card distribution)
- [ ] Print 500 discount cards: "20% off your first round — while you wait"
- [ ] Map target restaurants within 3-block radius (Coop 303, Flying Iguana, Poe's Tavern, Ocean 60)
- [ ] Initiate partnership outreach to at least 2 nearby restaurants
- [ ] Source ghost kitchen/catering partner for appetizers and small plates
- [ ] Draft ghost kitchen menu (5–8 items) with 45%+ margins
- [ ] Begin social media content calendar (4–5 posts/week on Instagram and TikTok)

### Week 4

- [ ] Launch wait-list redirect program — door staff distributing discount cards nightly
- [ ] Launch ghost kitchen food offering (soft launch, 3–4 nights/week)
- [ ] Schedule and host first daytime activation event (yoga, networking, or gaming tournament)
- [ ] Send first email newsletter to list (recap of new menu, events, birthday offer)
- [ ] **Milestone:** First week of wait-list program complete — report card redemptions

---

## Phase 3: Optimization & Scaling (Month 2–3)

### Month 2

- [ ] Launch paid Meta (Instagram/Facebook) ads — target 25–45 demo, 10-mile radius
- [ ] Weekly ad spend: $150–250/week minimum
- [ ] Scale food offering to 7 nights/week based on Month 1 data
- [ ] Negotiate formal partnership agreement with 1–2 nearby restaurants
- [ ] Establish recurring daytime events (weekly or bi-weekly)
- [ ] Grow Instagram followers to 1,000+
- [ ] Collect 15+ new Yelp/Google reviews via follow-up emails
- [ ] **Milestone:** Month 2 report — revenue impact vs. baseline

### Month 3

- [ ] Review and optimize ad creative and targeting based on Month 2 data
- [ ] Expand ghost kitchen to full service (all nights, full appetizer menu)
- [ ] Onboard second daytime event format
- [ ] Reach 50+ Yelp reviews milestone
- [ ] Instagram followers: 2,000+; engagement rate: 5%+
- [ ] Door staff card redemptions: 60–80/week
- [ ] **Milestone:** Month 3 full KPI review (see KPIs below)
