## 📋 The Four Revenue Streams

### 1. **Website Optimization** (Week 1)
- Upload cocktail menu with pricing
- Add 20+ professional photos
- Integrate Google & Yelp review widgets
- Link Instagram/TikTok
- Create event pages with times
- Setup email automation (birthday discounts)
- **Impact:** +$4.5K/month immediately

### 2. **Wait-List Redirect Program** (Week 3+)
- Train door staff as brand ambassadors
- Distribute discount cards to people waiting at nearby restaurants
- Partnerships with Coop 303, Flying Iguana, etc.
- Offer 20% off first round to wait-list customers
- **Impact:** +$12K–18K/month

### 3. **Ghost Kitchen Food Partnership** (Week 3+)
- Partner with local catering/food vendor
- Offer appetizers and small plates at 45% margin
- Increase average customer spend by $8–12
- **Impact:** +$8K–12K/month

### 4. **Digital Marketing & Daytime Activations** (Week 4+)
- Instagram/TikTok content calendar (4–5 posts/week)
- Paid ads targeting 25–45 demographic
- Daytime events: yoga, gaming tournaments, networking hours
- Partner with local businesses to drive attendance
- **Impact:** +$6K–10K/month (digital) + $4K–8K/month (events)
